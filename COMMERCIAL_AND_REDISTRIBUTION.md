# Commercial Use, Redistribution, and Special Permissions

The standard StainlessSteel license is **PolyForm Strict 1.0.0** for first-party code. To obtain rights not covered by it, secure **explicit written permission from the verified copyright holder** before proceeding.

## Examples requiring review

- Selling or distributing StainlessSteel, bundles, forks, derived binaries, app-store packages, or distro packages.
- Incorporating StainlessSteel into a proprietary product or offering it as a hosted/managed service.
- Using StainlessSteel as part of commercial work where PolyForm Strict does not permit the specific use.
- Publishing altered StainlessSteel code or granting sublicenses.
- Packaging StainlessSteel binaries in Homebrew, Linux repositories, mirrors or third-party installers, unless that distributor was explicitly authorized.

## Distribution contracts must clarify

- Licensed acts and precise scope, territory, duration and named versions.
- Attribution, license preservation, official update channels, and security support.
- No sublicensing, resale or onward distribution beyond what is expressly authorized.
- Termination, existing customers, and ongoing security updates.
- Treatment of source code, modifications, contributor rights and patent rights.
- Third-party components stay under their own licenses; the copyright holder cannot restrict the third-party license freedoms of those components.

**This is a permissions workflow, not an automatically binding contract. Have a software/IP lawyer draft the actual commercial agreement.**
